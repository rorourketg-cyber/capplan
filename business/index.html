CapPlan Business

Which edition is right
for your business?

Both editions include all 13 capital planning models. The difference is in how each handles tax depreciation — which depends on where your business files its taxes.

🇺🇸
US Edition

CapPlan Business
United States

Built for businesses that file US federal taxes. Uses the depreciation system mandated by the IRS, including first-year bonus depreciation elections.

US tax depreciation
MACRS — Modified Accelerated Cost Recovery System
Bonus depreciation (IRC §168(k))
All analysis in US dollars
🌍
International Edition

CapPlan Business
International

For businesses outside the US, or any jurisdiction that follows classical accounting depreciation methods rather than MACRS. Supports nine currencies.

Classical depreciation methods
Straight-line
Double declining balance
Sum-of-years digits
💱
Currency selector: The International Edition supports nine currencies — USD, CAD, GBP, EUR, AUD, NZD, CHF, JPY, and SGD — selectable within each model. The US Edition uses US dollars throughout.
