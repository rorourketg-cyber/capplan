About this suite

What's included and how it works.

13 models covering every major capital decision. Each model is built for one specific decision type — no guesswork, no general-purpose spreadsheet.

US tax-aware throughout. Every model that involves depreciation uses MACRS (Modified Accelerated Cost Recovery System), with full support for bonus depreciation and Section 179 expensing.

Asset Purchase Analysis is free permanently. It is always available, with or without a subscription.

Your data stays with you. All calculations run in your browser. Nothing is sent to our servers.
