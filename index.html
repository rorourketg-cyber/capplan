How it works

From question to answer
in three steps

CapPlan turns complex capital planning calculations into guided, plain-language analyses anyone can run.

01
Choose your model
Browse the full model list or use the built-in navigator to find the right tool for your decision. Each model is described in plain language so you always know exactly what you're running.
02
Enter your numbers
Each model walks you through inputs with clear labels and guidance on where to find the data. Built-in estimators handle figures that are hard to know precisely. Load an example to see how it works first.
03
Understand the answer
Results include a clear recommendation, sensitivity analysis showing how the answer shifts under different assumptions, and full DCF workings. Every result explains not just what, but why.
